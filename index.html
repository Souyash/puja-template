Who We Are

Not just a club —
a family since 1975

Kallol of New Jersey was founded on April 13th, 1975 — Pohela Boishakh. We are a full-spectrum community organization: we celebrate culture, compete in sports, run blood drives and community programs, and stand by each other through every season of life.

We are not just a religious or cultural organization. We are your neighbours, your team-mates, your family away from home.

Our Timeline

Five decades
of history

From 24 families gathering on a Bengali New Year's Day in Edison, to one of the most celebrated South Asian cultural organizations in the tri-state area.

1975 — Founded
Founded on Pohela Boishakh by 24 members. First Bijoya Sammilanee held at Glenfield Park, Montclair.
1976 — Literary Roots
First quarterly newsletter and Kallol Sahitya Patrika literary magazine launched to preserve Bengali letters.
1977 — Durga Puja Begins
Launched NJ Durga Puja — now the flagship annual celebration drawing thousands of devotees and families.
2026 — 50 Years Strong
Celebrating five decades of culture, philanthropy, and community leadership in New Jersey.
